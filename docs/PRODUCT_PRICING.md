# Licensing boundaries — no public price launch

The base Shop + WhatsApp + PWA remains independent of recurring software licenses. Existing product prices continue to belong to the bakery and may be unknown; no zero-price placeholder or fabricated final total was added.

Orders Pro, Food Pro, Group Delivery, Club and Reviews are optional entitlements enforced by the server. Food Pro includes Orders Pro, rather than charging twice. Group Delivery and Club do not depend on kitchen software. This pilot has no checkout for HASI products, subscription billing or monthly price publication.

The attached master plan's commercial prices are drafts/background. No monthly commercial price was activated or published by this implementation. License references record a manual authorization, not a payment receipt. Future HASI billing/pricing must be injected through an adapter after owner approval.

Group-delivery customer amounts use server catalog snapshots plus the known/maximum agreed fee. Unknown-price products cannot enter prepaid group requests. Free delivery creates pending manual-refund records for previously paid fees; only actual, referenced refunds become verified. Route costs are private manual estimates and are not called net profit.
